title: Grievances
region: global
topic: conduct
---

File a grievance in writing within 30 days of the decision you are challenging.
The People team acknowledges a written grievance within 5 working days.
A grievance may challenge a performance rating or another workplace decision.
Appeal the outcome to the Managing Director within 10 working days of the outcome.
The People team decides the grievance. The handbook assistant does not decide it.
