The home-office stipend in the United States is 500 USD and can be claimed once every 3 years.
The home-office stipend in the United Kingdom is 400 GBP and can be claimed once every 3 years.
The home-office stipend in Germany is 450 EUR and can be claimed once every 3 years.
A laptop is provided by IT and is not bought with the home-office stipend.
Return equipment within 10 days of leaving.
