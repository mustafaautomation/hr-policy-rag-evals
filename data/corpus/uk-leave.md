title: Annual leave in the United Kingdom
region: uk
topic: leave
---

Full-time employees in the United Kingdom receive 28 days of annual leave per leave year, and that total includes 8 bank holidays.
Annual leave requests go to the manager.
Employees in the United Kingdom may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days with manager approval, and those days must be used by 31 March.
