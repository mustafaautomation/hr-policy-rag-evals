title: Paid time off in the United States
region: us
topic: leave
---

Full-time employees in the United States receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year.
Employees in the United States may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year.
PTO is for rest, appointments, and personal time.
Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount of paid time off.
