The United States medical plan is Northbridge Health.
Cedarline Studio pays 80% of the employee premium and 60% of the dependent premium on the United States plan.
The United States HSA contribution is $1000 per year for an individual and $2000 per year for a family.
United States health coverage starts on the first day of the month after the start date.
