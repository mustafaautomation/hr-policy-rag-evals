Flights under 6 hours are booked in Economy.
Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.
The hotel cap in Germany is 180 EUR per night.
The hotel cap in the United Kingdom is 200 GBP per night.
The hotel cap in the United States is 220 USD per night.
The meal allowance in the United Kingdom is 50 GBP per day.
The meal allowance in Germany is 55 EUR per day.
The meal allowance in the United States is 60 USD per day.
Book travel through the company travel desk.
