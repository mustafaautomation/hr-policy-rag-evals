Hybrid employees may work remotely for up to 3 days each week.
Core hours are 10:00-15:00 in the office timezone.
Working from a country other than the country of employment for more than 10 working days in a year needs People team approval.
Remote work is available after 3 months of employment.
A fully remote arrangement exists only when the offer letter says so.
